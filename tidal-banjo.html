Fundy Tidal

← Back to Models

A standardized open-back banjo built for old-time and clawhammer playing — the anchor of the Fundy lineup.

The Tidal offers boutique-level quality and setup in a refined, repeatable design. By standardizing the design, it reduces the time and complexity of a fully custom build while maintaining the same quality of materials, playability, and craftsmanship.

Offered with either an 11″ or 12″ rim — the 11″ is lighter and more focused, while the 12″ offers greater low-end response and a fuller voice. Every Tidal is set up with careful attention to neck geometry, string height, and playability before it leaves the shop.

The Tidal is not an entry-level instrument. It is the baseline expression of Fundy Banjos.
