Fundy Tuner App Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

What the app does

Fundy Tuner is an instrument-tuning app. It uses your device microphone to analyze the sound of your instrument and display pitch information.

Audio and personal data

Microphone audio is processed on your device in real time for pitch detection. Fundy Tuner does not upload, sell, or retain your audio. The app does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or other personal information.

Optional diagnostics

If you deliberately enable diagnostics, the app can create a local debug report containing technical events such as timestamps and device information. The report remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export it. Fundy Tuner does not automatically send diagnostic reports.

Network access and third parties

Fundy Tuner does not use network services for tuning and does not share user data with third parties. The app contains no advertising or tracking.

Children

Fundy Tuner is a general-purpose music tool and is not directed specifically to children. Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app’s data practices change. Questions can be sent to fundybanjos@gmail.com.
