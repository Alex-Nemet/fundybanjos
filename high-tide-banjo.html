Fundy High Tide

← Back to Models

A premium configuration of the Tidal with upgraded materials and undercut frets — fret ends are not exposed, giving the neck a bound feel without binding.

The High Tide builds on the Tidal platform with upgraded neck woods and undercut frets. Where the Tidal uses through-cut frets, the High Tide frets are undercut into the fingerboard so the fret ends are not exposed along the edge of the neck — similar to having binding, but without it.

Offered with either an 11″ or 12″ rim. Neck options include maple, walnut, or torrified maple. A small number of custom details may be added, such as engraved scoop plates or custom headstock overlays — these are quoted individually depending on the work involved.
